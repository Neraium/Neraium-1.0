# Executive technical summary

## What was demonstrated

The frozen Neraium system completed a chronological retrospective analysis of published real full-scale wastewater telemetry: **74,171 observations, 253 windows, 21 relationships and 5,265 pair-window evaluations**. Each comparison used a preceding fixed reference and retained chronological state; production analytical logic, thresholds and governance were unchanged.

All four promoted states reproduced in **12 same-process and four fresh-process reruns**. Complete target relationship edges, governed SII evidence, insights, evidence index, supplied-reference provenance and outgoing states matched exactly. Inputs, outputs, configuration, differences and hashes are retained.

Complete outputs were not identical. Execution timestamps, generated run identifiers, timing, memory and performance metadata legitimately differed. Strict complete-output matches and the narrower prespecified comparison both remained **0/12 and 0/4**; analytical-component equality must not be presented as complete-output identity.

## What was observed but not independently validated

**505 pair-window evaluations had graph temporal support. Four observations across NH4/NO3 and NO3/N2O retained governed persistent-change flags after promotion.** These are repeated statistical-evidence observations, not independently confirmed plant events, fault detections or accuracy results.

All windows retained operating-context limitations. Measurable consequence remained not quantifiable. No independently documented plant-event correspondence was established. Graph/governed persistence flags coexist with limited/false insight-level persistence, and the saved evidence does not fully expose the distinguishing promotion-decision predicate.

## What remains unproven

Event correctness, accuracy/error rates, physical or economic benefit, emissions reduction, causal attribution, generalization to other plants and prospective field performance remain unproven. Source gaps, finite but questionable measurements, initial constant readings, unknown units/timezone and the choice of a single reference/window policy limit interpretation. Reproducibility of selected calls does not resolve those limitations.

The earlier controlled campaign is a separate software-validation evidence stream. Its synthetic controls and designed perturbations cannot establish real-wastewater accuracy, and its denominators are not pooled with this dataset.
