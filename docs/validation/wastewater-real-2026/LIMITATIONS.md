# Limitations and interpretation boundaries

- This is retrospective analysis of a user-supplied wastewater record. It does not establish deployment, prospective performance, prediction, predictive maintenance, diagnosis, root cause, prescribed action or algorithmic novelty. No independent event labels support accuracy, sensitivity, specificity or false-positive estimates.
- The source contains no units, timezone, plant identifier, calibration history, event logs or sampling-method metadata. External documentation may contextualize the record only after the analytical freeze; it cannot retroactively strengthen blind inputs or qualification.
- The first seven days are a fixed observed reference, not verified normal or healthy operation. Initial NH4 includes a long zero-valued stretch. Seasonal and changing operating conditions may differ from that reference. No alternative-baseline search was performed.
- All seven signals are analyzed under the existing generic numeric relationship semantics. Absence of usable operating-state/context signals limits like-for-like comparison. A supported correlation change does not establish an unexplained plant condition.
- One nonnumeric flow cell is excluded through the existing historical wrapper's complete-case policy. Negative finite flow, zero values, constant stretches and outliers are retained. Numeric validity is not sensor validity. Wrapper numeric profiles describe retained rows; the separate quality audit must accompany results.
- Six source gaps remain unfilled. Daily bins contain different sample counts; the final bin is partial. Persistence support is based on observed windows and does not prove unobserved continuity across gaps.
- Graph temporal support, graph promotion, recurrence and governed findings are distinct outputs. A graph persistence flag alone is not an unrestricted systemic finding. Counts of pair-window support are repeated observations, not independent incidents or confirmed anomalies.
- Daily aggregation of evidence can miss shorter behavior. Only one prespecified reference/window policy was evaluated; no robustness claim across baseline or window choices follows. Reference rows recur in engine calls and must not be counted as new independent observations.
- Correlations are dimensionless and observational. Autocorrelation, shared drivers, mathematical dependence between source variables, rounding, censoring and measurement issues can affect relationships. No causal or physical-benefit interpretation follows from a correlation displacement.
- Quantified emissions, resource losses, cost, savings or benefit require valid units, a supported measurement model and legitimate attribution. Source variable names alone do not supply those requirements.
- A single dataset and facility cannot establish generalization to other plants. User-described novelty of the input is not independently auditable model-training provenance.
- Runtime on this shared EC2 environment is descriptive. It is not a throughput SLA, isolated benchmark or cloud-production assurance. Earlier controlled validation reported separate performance and replay limitations; those artifacts are unchanged.
- Earlier controlled testing exposed dictionary-order sensitivity in operating-context evidence. This package preserves source-column and dictionary order and scopes repeatability comparisons explicitly. Hash equality alone does not cure that production limitation.
- Checkpoint hashes are local integrity evidence, not independent attestation. The wrapper and engine are separate checkouts; both code identities and dependency versions are required for reproduction. Untracked validation artifacts make the working tree dirty even though production source hashes remain unchanged.
- Raw engine responses may contain generic investigation, consequence or heuristic language. They are preserved for audit, not adopted as validated claims. External-facing text is restricted to supported retrospective evidence.
- Eight daily windows contain constant NH4 and yield only 15 of 21 possible correlations. The 48 omitted pair-window slots are unavailable, not negative controls. The relationship-analysis module still reports complete; coverage must be interpreted separately from module status.
- External metadata gives 74,173 observations, while the byte-identical file has 74,172 data rows plus its header. The discrepancy is retained rather than silently adopting the metadata count. Full publication access returned HTTP 403, and no independently timed operating-event correspondence was established.
- The four promoted/governed persistence appearances are isolated in consecutive-promotion bookkeeping. Their retained evidence includes earlier supporting observations, but no uninterrupted physical change duration is established. The broader 505 temporal-supported observations must not be represented as 505 governed findings.

## Closure addendum: limits after targeted repeatability

The original limitations above remain applicable. Four promoted observations were subsequently rerun three times each in one process and once each in separate fresh processes. All promoted states and the listed analytical components reproduced in all 16 calls. This addresses reproducibility of these selected original-state evaluations under the recorded environment and ordering; it does not establish general determinism across environments, input-order variants, full chronological replays or other plants.

Complete-output and prespecified two-field-excluded comparisons remained 0/12 for same-process and 0/4 for fresh-process calls. Condition-timeline execution timestamps remained different; full responses also differed in generated run IDs and timing/memory/performance metadata. Those legitimate execution differences remain disclosed. The criteria were not broadened to claim an exact-output pass.

All windows still lack sufficient operating context. Insight-level persistence remains limited/false despite the graph/governed persistent flags, and measurable consequence remains not quantifiable. No independent plant-event correspondence or accuracy result was established. The missing per-edge change-promotion decision trace, original source-quality limitations, gaps, unknown units/timezone and unvalidated physical attribution remain unresolved.

Original report versions are preserved under history/pre-closure/. Raw evidence is immutable and recoverable from the checksummed retention archives; no raw output or previously disclosed failure was removed.
